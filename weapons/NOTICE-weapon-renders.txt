The icons in this folder are renders of Red Faction's own weapon and ammo models (the pickup meshes in meshes.vpp). RomekRF gives away everything that is his in them, the rendering and the styling, with no conditions (CC0 for his part). He cannot license the underlying designs, which belong to Volition / THQ Nordic: these icons stand on the same footing as any other Red Faction mod asset and are meant for use in Red Faction mods and clients that need the game. The clean alternative is ../renderer/ (MPL-2.0): render the icons from the player's own game files instead of shipping these. This is a plain description, not legal advice.
